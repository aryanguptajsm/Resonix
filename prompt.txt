- Hot cues.

5. DUAL DECK AUDIO ENGINE
==================================================

This is the most important feature.

Build two independent audio decks.

Each deck must support:

- Load audio files.
- Play.
- Pause.
- Stop.
- Seek.
- Current playback time.
- Total duration.
- Volume.
- Playback speed.
- Pitch adjustment.
- Independent EQ.
- Independent audio effects.

Supported formats should depend on browser and
desktop decoder support.

Prioritize:
- MP3
- WAV
- OGG
- FLAC where supported.

Create a proper audio architecture.

Each deck should have its own signal chain:

Audio Source
    |
Gain Control
    |
3-Band EQ
    |
Effects
    |
Channel Gain
    |
Crossfader
    |
Master Gain
    |
Audio Output

Both decks must connect to a shared master mixer.

Prevent duplicate AudioContext connections.

Handle audio errors properly.

Do not create an audio player that only looks functional.
All controls must actually work.
